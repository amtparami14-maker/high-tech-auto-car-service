1 Channel Relay Module 5V / 10A

A relay is an electrically operated switch. It allows a low-power control circuit to switch another electrical load.

Relay Module Pins

  • VCC – Power supply
  • GND – Ground
  • IN – Control input
  • COM – Common contact
  • NO – Normally Open
  • NC – Normally Closed

How It Works

When the control input activates the relay, the internal electromagnetic mechanism changes the contact position.

COM can connect to NO when the relay is activated, while NC is connected to COM when the relay is in its normal state.

Typical Applications

  • Motors
  • Lamps
  • Fans
  • Pumps
  • Automotive electrical projects
  • IoT projects
  • Microcontroller projects

Relay modules are useful when a controller such as Arduino, ESP32 or another low-voltage control circuit needs to control a higher-power load.
